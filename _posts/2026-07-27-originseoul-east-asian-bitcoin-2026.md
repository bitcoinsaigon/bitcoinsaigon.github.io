After a comparatively quiet first half of 2026, Bitcoin communities across East and Southeast Asia are springing back into action.

Just days after the large-scale Bitcoin Asia 2026 conference in Hong Kong \- organized by BTC Inc., the company behind Bitcoin Magazine \- a decidedly Bitcoin-only gathering will take place in Seoul.

Our friends in the South Korean capital are back with ORIGINSEOUL 2026, a three-day conference under a new banner. [ORIGIN X founder Ryan](https://x.com/ryanbitcoiner) previously founded and ran BitcoinSeoul for two years before stepping away from that event. With ORIGINSEOUL, the team aims to strengthen a distinctly Bitcoin-first culture in Korea and connect the country’s community more closely with the wider Asian Bitcoin ecosystem.

![](/assets/images/originseoul-east-asian-bitcoin-2026-1.webp)

Not everyone in the Bitcoin community is a fan of the more commercial mega-events, such as the one in Hong Kong, with thousands of attendees and \- by necessity of the economics at that scale \- a more commercial character. Smaller conferences with deep roots in their communities provide a more intimate setting to get to know some of the people involved in and contributing to Bitcoin and its proliferation in various ways \- whether in their respective local communities or among a wider global audience.

Visitors will also likely encounter at least one or two more familiar faces from the Saigon and broader Vietnamese Bitcoin community in Seoul.

**When:** August 31 \- September 2, 2026

**Where:** Seoul, South Korea

**What:** Three-day Bitcoin-only conference

**August 31:** VIP Day \- [Bitcoin House Origin](https://www.btchouse.kr/)

**September 1:** Industry Day \- SKY31 Convention, Lotte World Tower, 31F

**September 2:** Public Day \- SKY31 Convention, Lotte World Tower, 31F

[**GET YOUR TICKETS HERE**](https://originseoulbtc.com/#pass)

If you will already be in Asia at the time and are looking to build closer connections with local Bitcoin communities from across the region \- or simply already have Seoul on your travel itinerary \- you now have one more reason to make that trip happen soon.
